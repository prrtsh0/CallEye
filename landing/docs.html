CallEye docs

A calm, honest pressure-call reader. This page documents how it works, what it can and cannot do, and how it was evaluated.

What CallEye is

CallEye listens to a call that feels off and tells you — in a calm colour — whether it shows scam signs. It is a prototype for a student challenge, not a law-enforcement tool and not a guarantee.

How it works

CallEye reads the shape of a call, not the truth. It looks for five pressure patterns scammers rely on:

  • Authority — "I am from the police / your bank."
  • Fear — arrest, a blocked account, legal trouble.
  • Isolation — "don't tell anyone", stay on the line.
  • Urgency — "right now", a deadline, the only option.
  • Ask — OTP, money transfer, an app install.

These are weighted into a 0–100 pressure reading with a trend. A call that asks for something a bank or the police would never ask for (OTP, PIN, CVV, "safe account", paying to avoid arrest) is treated as a serious red flag.

The verdict levels

  • Green — no warning signs so far. CallEye never says "safe".
  • Amber — careful, check before you act. Emergency claims land here by default.
  • Red — hang up; your guardian is told.

Privacy & limits

Privacy: no audio and no transcript are stored.

Limits: it works on speakerphone, can be wrong, and cannot hang up for you. It is not a substitute for checking with a number you already have.
